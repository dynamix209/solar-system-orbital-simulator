Orbital inclination
Planet orbit lines are tilted relative to the ecliptic (Earth’s orbital plane, i = 0°).
• Warm / redder lines = higher tilt (e.g. Mercury ~7°)
• Cool / brass lines = nearly flat (Earth ~0°)
• Vertical ticks mark the tilt angle from the ecliptic plane.
Toggle with “Inclination ON/OFF” in the dock.
